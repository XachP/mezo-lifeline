Lifeline · liquidation + redemption protection for MUSD1 / 11

Two ways an MUSD borrower loses BTC

Liquidation

  • Under 110% ICR, or under 150% in Recovery Mode
  • BTC falls 20–30% in days
  • Today's defense is to top up by hand at 3 AM

Redemption

  • MUSD is swapped for BTC from the lowest-ICR troves ≥ 110% first
  • Live testnet today: the front of the queue is at 114–131%
  • At 127% ICR, only ~18.6k MUSD of debt stands in front of you

Fear of both keeps BTC holders from borrowing MUSD at all.

Lifeline · liquidation + redemption protection for MUSD2 / 11

The solution

LifelineVault: a smart account that is the borrower of record for one MUSD trove.

The owner parks a small BTC and/or MUSD rescue reserve and sets a policy:
trigger · target · keeper tip (≤ 2%, capped) · redemption buffer

protect() is permissionless. It fires when ICR < trigger or the trove is inside the redemption buffer:

  • adds BTC (addColl) or repays (repayMUSD)
  • lifts the trove to safety, again and again
  • pays the caller a capped tip

Keepers can only move the reserve into the owner's own trove. Withdrawals, borrowing and closing stay owner-only. Works for smart accounts; no one-shot pre-signed signatures.

Why it grows MUSD

  • More borrowing: holders who fear liquidation and redemption can borrow with a standing safety net
  • Distribution: an open-source keeper with Telegram alerts meets users where they already are (Track 2)
  • Composable: built on the deployed mUSD contracts, with no protocol changes
  • Revenue path: an optional, owner-chosen service fee on rescues; keepers earn tips
  • Wave 2: auto-deleverage through a Mezo pool (no idle reserve), MUSD savings yield on the reserve, one-click migration of existing troves, a hosted alert bot, then mainnet after review
