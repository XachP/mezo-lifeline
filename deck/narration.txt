Lifeline. A standing safety policy for MUSD troves on Mezo. Set one rule, and anyone can enforce it, but no one can abuse it.
MUSD borrowers can lose Bitcoin two ways: liquidation under one hundred ten percent, and redemption, which takes Bitcoin from the lowest ratio troves first, even at one twenty seven.
A Lifeline vault owns your trove and holds a small rescue reserve. When your ratio drops below your trigger, or you drift to the front of the redemption queue, anyone can call protect. Keepers can only move your reserve into your own trove.
It plugs straight into the deployed mUSD contracts and the sorted trove list. It raises the trigger in Recovery Mode, and uses exact hints, which cut gas from six million to under seven hundred thousand.
