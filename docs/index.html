Lifeline

A standing safety policy for MUSD troves on Mezo. Park a small BTC or MUSD rescue reserve in your vault. When BTC drops below your trigger, or your trove drifts to the front of mUSD's redemption queue, any keeper can push it back to safety, again and again, with exact sorted-list hints. Keepers can only move your reserve into your own trove, and they earn a tip you cap. Works with smart accounts; no pre-signed one-shot signatures.

2. Redemption queue radar (live Mezo testnet, no wallet)

mUSD lets anyone swap MUSD for BTC from the lowest-ICR troves at or above 110% first. You are not liquidated, but you lose BTC at today's price. Enter an ICR to see how much other debt stands between you and the next redemption.
